# Returns & Refunds Policy — Voice Shop

## Return window
- Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Perishable goods, gift cards, and clearance items marked "final sale" are not returnable.

## Condition requirements
- Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with tags attached where applicable.
- Opened electronics can be returned within 15 days if all original accessories and packaging are included.

## How refunds are issued
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of the returned item being received and inspected.
- Orders placed as Cash on Delivery are refunded via store credit or bank transfer, customer's choice.

## Exchanges
- Direct exchanges (e.g. size or color swap) are processed as a return plus a new order, since Voice Shop does not yet support in-place exchanges.

## Damaged or incorrect items
- If an item arrives damaged or is not what was ordered, customers are entitled to a full refund or free replacement — no return shipping cost to the customer in this case.
- Damaged/incorrect item claims must be reported within 7 days of delivery.
