# Payment Policy — Voice Shop

## Accepted payment modes
- Cash on Delivery (COD) — the default and currently only checkout mode supported by the Voice Shop demo storefront.
- Credit/debit card and digital wallet support is planned for a future release.

## Charges and fees
- No surcharge is applied for Cash on Delivery orders under $200.
- Orders over $200 paid via COD carry a $3 handling fee, collected at delivery.

## Order changes and cancellations
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time before they are marked "packed."
- Once an order is out for delivery, it can no longer be cancelled but can be refused at the door, which triggers the standard refund process.

## Pricing
- All prices shown are in USD and inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.
- Prices can change without notice, but the price shown at the time of order placement is the price honored for that order.
