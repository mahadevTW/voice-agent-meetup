# Warranty Policy — Voice Shop

## Standard warranty
- All electronics and appliances carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty against defects, starting from the delivery date.
- Non-electronic items (apparel, home goods, groceries) are not covered by a manufacturer warranty but remain eligible under the standard returns policy.

## What's covered
- Manufacturing defects, malfunction under normal use, and dead-on-arrival units.
- Dead-on-arrival units reported within 48 hours of delivery are replaced free of charge, no questions asked.

## What's not covered
- Accidental damage, water damage, unauthorized repairs, or normal wear and tear.
- Damage caused by using the item outside its intended purpose.

## How to claim
- Customers should provide their order ID and a description of the issue.
- Voice Shop will arrange a free pickup for warranty-covered items and issue a replacement or refund once the item is inspected.
