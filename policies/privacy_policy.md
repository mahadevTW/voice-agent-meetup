# Privacy Policy — Voice Shop

## What we collect
- Order details (items, quantities, totals) and delivery information needed to fulfil an order.
- Voice transcripts from support conversations, used only to improve support quality.

## What we don't do
- Voice Shop does not sell customer data to third parties.
- Voice Shop does not require account creation or store payment card details — the demo storefront uses a single shared cart and Cash on Delivery only.

## Data retention
- Order history is retained for 24 months for support and warranty purposes, then anonymized.
- Voice transcripts are retained for 90 days.

## Customer rights
- Customers can request a copy of their order history or deletion of their voice transcripts at any time by asking a support agent.
